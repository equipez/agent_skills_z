# Sources and attribution

This shared skill adapts Cunxin Huang's personal scientific-writing workflow.
Its nine proof and derivation guidelines were provided by Zaikun Zhang. The
remaining material is an AI-assisted synthesis of writing guidance, original
examples, and practical LaTeX review procedures. It is a contributed workflow
for discussion, not an official statement from the authors or publishers of
the references below.

## Public sources

- Donald E. Knuth, Tracy Larrabee, and Paul M. Roberts, *Mathematical Writing*.
  Use the Stanford course report based on CS209 (1987), particularly §1,
  printed pp. 1–6, and §31, printed pp. 69–71.
  [Author's page](https://cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/klr.html);
  [Stanford report](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/papers/cs1193.pdf).
- Nicholas J. Higham, *Handbook of Writing for the Mathematical Sciences*,
  second edition, SIAM, 1998. Main sections: §§3.1–3.9, 6.1–6.2, 6.6,
  6.8–6.12, 6.14, 7.1, and 7.5.
  [Publisher record](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/book/10.1137/1.9780898719550).
  Page references in `book-guidance.md` refer to this edition.
- Tom M. Ragonneau and Zaikun Zhang, *PDFO: a cross-platform package for
  Powell's derivative-free optimization solvers*, *Mathematical Programming
  Computation*, 16, 535–559, 2024. DOI: 10.1007/s12532-024-00257-9.
  [Open manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.13246v8).
  The dated October 10, 2024 manuscript is the exemplar used here: §1 gives
  the practical problem and concrete contributions; §3.1.2 motivates the
  interpolation representation; §4.5 explains a safeguard and its caveat;
  §5 states the experimental questions before setup and findings.
- Larry McEnerney, [LEADERSHIP LAB: The Craft of Writing Effectively](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtIzMaLkCaM).
  Useful ranges: 00:04–00:08 (discovery versus presentation), 00:13–00:16
  (value for specific readers), 00:54–01:02 (problem and consequences), and
  01:03–01:19 (literature review and problem construction). The contributor
  reviewed the public English automatic-caption transcript; this does not
  claim a complete audio/video review or access to the separate handout.
  [UChicago's account of the teaching](https://news.uchicago.edu/story/how-one-scholar-shaped-writing-generations-students)
  provides institutional context.

## How the sources are used

The skill contains operational paraphrases, the shared proof guidelines, and
original worked examples. Full books, lecture transcripts, private messages,
and manuscript files are not required or bundled. Read the selected local
reference first; consult a cited work only when the task needs its detail.

Use the rules to make purpose, assumptions, and logical progression visible.
Do not copy another paper's findings into a new manuscript, treat a numerical
observation as a theorem, or imitate isolated adjectives from an exemplar.
Bibliographic sources do not establish the correctness of the user's claims.
